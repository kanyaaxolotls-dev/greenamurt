About FutureLife

Bringing the wisdom of Ayurveda to every Indian home — for health, wealth & safety.

Who We Are

GA Harbal Pvt Ltd, based in Saswad, Pune, is the company behind the FutureLife wellness brand — rooted in ancient Ayurvedic traditions and committed to providing pure, natural and effective health products for every Indian family.

Through our trusted brand Green Amrut and sister brands, we offer a comprehensive range of Ayurvedic products — from immunity-boosting juices and joint care powders to diabetes management and women's wellness solutions.

Our mission is simple: Health Today, Wealth Tomorrow, Safety Always. We believe that true wellness encompasses physical health, financial security and protection for your loved ones.

  • Founded on the principles of ancient Ayurvedic science
  • 18+ products covering major health categories
  • 100% natural ingredients — no harmful chemicals
  • GMP certified manufacturing facilities
  • Serving thousands of families across India
  • Affordable pricing for every household
